Constructed entirely with a full floating canvas harvested from Siberian horsehair and Mongolian cashmere felt. No thermal fusing or glued substrates will ever touch an Atelier Vanguard commission.

  • 3.75-inch hand-padded lapel roll calibrated for lifetime memory.
  • Individual cut utilizing 14-inch Sheffield hot-forged steel shears.
  • 2.5-inch internal inlay seam allowance for multi-generational resizing.
  • Hand-sewn horn buttons with cross-shank anchoring thread.

Milled in the pristine alpine waters of Biella, Italy, the Super 150s Tasmania wool weighs an agile 280g/m—providing fluid four-season drape and rapid wrinkle resurgence.

Internal breast facings incorporate authentic handloom Jamdani silk woven on pit looms along the Shitalakshya River in Narayanganj, integrating three centuries of Bengal sovereign textile supremacy.

We assume full fiscal liability for your posture and drape. If your initial garment requires adjustments, you enjoy complimentary anatomical refinements at our Savile Row, Madison Avenue, or Gulshan-2 salons.

For patrons remote from our physical ateliers, Atelier Vanguard reimburses up to $150 USD with your approved local master tailor upon receipt submission.

Every finished two-piece suit is conditioned, brushed with natural boar bristle, draped on custom hornbeam timber hangers, and sealed in an aromatic Spanish cedar travel trunk.

Dispatched via temperature-controlled, fully bonded and insured international courier (DDP with all duties and tariffs cleared by our Mayfair treasury).

BESPOKE PEDIGREE

The Anatomical Anatomy of the Drape

An unyielding refusal to compromise: seven hallmarks that separate true continental bespoke from assembly-line pretension.

01 / SEVEN architecture

The Floating Siberian Canvas

Suspended freely between outer wool and inner lining, horsehair and camel hair fibers mold to the unique contours of the wearer's chest over 30 days of wear, creating an inimitable custom mold.

02 / SEVEN straighten

Sovereign High Armhole Cut

Carved with razor-close anatomical proximity to the axilla. This Savile Row standard permits complete 180-degree arm articulation without pulling or displacing the jacket's collar from the neck.

03 / SEVEN fingerprint

Milanese Hand Buttonhole

Known as the 'Asola Lucida'. Hand-sewn over a waxed silk gimp cord for 45 minutes per buttonhole, creating an architectural relief that no computerized machinery can simulate.

04 / SEVEN texture

Artisanal Bengal Jamdani Facing

Ancestral supplementary weft technique woven thread-by-thread on bamboo pit looms. Breathable organic silk provides thermal balance and subtle heritage luster upon garment disclosure.

05 / SEVEN content_cut

Generous Seam Inlays

Up to 2.5 inches of virgin cloth folded within the seams. Should your stature shift across decades, our ateliers can comfortably let out or take in the waist, chest, and sleeves seamlessly.

06 / SEVEN iron

Curved Drape Iron-Pressing

Undergoing 12 individual pressing operations with 16-pound dry irons to shape flat two-dimensional worsted cloth into the three-dimensional barrel curvature of the human torso.

07 / SEVEN — MASTER BENCHMARK

The 88-Hour Human Threshold

Zero shortcuts. Every Mayfair Royal Drape commission represents exactly 88 hours of individual master artisan labor, recorded in our London ledger and verified by serial basting registry.
