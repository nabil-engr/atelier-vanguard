## Brand & Style
The design system articulates an aesthetic of quiet luxury, master tailoring, and continental poise. It blends the architectural discipline of Savile Row with the relaxed sprezzatura of Milanese cut, enriched by the artisanal heritage of Bengal’s finest weaves and Super 150s worsted wools.

The experience addresses an international patron base spanning Dhaka, Mayfair, Manhattan, and the 8th Arrondissement. The interface must evoke the atmosphere of an appointment-only private salon: deliberate pacing, unhurried negative space, tactile material presence, and razor-sharp typographic discipline. 

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Editorial Minimalism with Skeuomorphic Sartorial Micro-Details**:
- Vast, generous ivory fields that frame garment photography as fine art.
- Restrained, hair-thin divider lines reminiscent of tailor’s basting threads and chalk guides.
- Impeccable contrast between archival serifs and clinical modernist body typography.
- Precision interactions that mimic manual measurement, fabric drape inspection, and bespoke configuration.

## Colors
The palette captures the materials of a master cutter’s cutting table: midnight wool serge, aged brass shears, chalk dust, and warm unbleached silk lining.

- **Deep Midnight Navy (`#0B1623`)**: Primary anchor. Used for high-impact typography, primary action surfaces, deep background contrast panels, and structural boundaries.
- **Warm Ivory (`#F6F1E8`)**: Base neutral canvas. Replaces sterile digital white across primary application backgrounds to provide the organic, archival feel of heavy vellum and raw silk.
- **Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure highlight token used selectively for swatch backings, active card elevations, and crisp focus states.
- **Restrained Antique Gold (`#B39156`)**: Precious accent. Used strictly for subtle focus accents, active monograms, measurement calipers, and delicate 1px rule highlights. Never applied to large surface fills.
- **Muted Stone (`#A59E92`)**: Structural boundary neutral. Used for hairline rules, deactivated states, secondary captions, and architectural boundaries.
- **Charcoal Wool (`#252525`)**: High-legibility text neutral for continuous long-form editorial reading against ivory canvases.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm sets the tone of luxury editorial curation. The contrast relies on Playfair Display for narrative authority and high-fashion display, paired with the geometric clarity of Plus Jakarta Sans for functional information density.

- **Editorial Headings**: Set titles in sentence case or classical Roman title casing. Do not set Playfair Display in all-caps across extended sentences.
- **Uppercase Labels**: All system metadata, spec badges, swatch classifications, step counters, and category tags must be set using `label-uppercase` with an expanded letter-spacing of `0.18em`.
- **Text Alignment**: Never center-align body text beyond two lines. Hero descriptions, tailoring manifestos, and garment dossiers align strictly to the left edge of the grid column.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres strictly to an architectural 12-column responsive grid aligned with Bootstrap 5 and Angular layout abstractions:
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 1.5rem (`24px`) margins. Fluid full-bleed vertical layout.
- **Tablet (768px–1024px)**: 8 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, 3rem (`48px`) margins.
- **Desktop (1025px–1440px)**: 12 columns, 2rem (`32px`) gutters, 5rem (`80px`) margins. Maximum container max-width: `1360px`.
- **Large Desktop / Salon View (>1440px)**: 12 columns pinned to `1440px` centered canvas with expansive negative outer margins.

Vertical rhythm relies on generous section pacing:
- Major thematic transitions receive `space-xl` (56px) to `space-xl * 2` (112px).
- Atelier cards and garment spec layouts preserve internal padding of `space-lg` (32px).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system is conveyed through tonal layering, physical material boundaries, and razor-sharp structural lines rather than heavy simulated drop shadows.

- **The Basting Line (Primary Divider)**: Separation between modules, tiers, and navigation bars is handled by 1px solid lines tinted to Muted Stone at 30% opacity (`rgba(165, 158, 146, 0.3)`) or Antique Gold at 25% opacity (`rgba(179, 145, 86, 0.25)`).
- **Surface Layering**: 
  - Base level: Warm Ivory (`#F6F1E8`).
  - Active Surface / Elevated Card: Pure Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Overlay / Drawer / Bespoke Fitting Panel: Deep Midnight Navy (`#0B1623`).
- **Tactile Shadows (Restricted)**: Shadows are used solely for hovering swatches, floating measurement overlays, or active configurator popouts:
  - `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 22, 35, 0.08)` (Subtle, highly diffused, warm-tinted ambient shadow).
- **Backdrop Frost**: Modal sheets and drawer scrims employ `background: rgba(11, 22, 35, 0.6)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.

## Shapes
The shape language is strictly **Sharp (`roundedness: 0`)**. 

In high bespoke tailoring, luxury is signaled by precision cuts, straight architectural lines, and razor-sharp seams. Curved pill-buttons and soft border-radii conflict with the sartorial heritage of handcrafted suiting.
- All buttons, input fields, modal dialogs, swatch containers, and garment dossiers use `border-radius: 0px`.
- Circular forms are permitted solely for textile swatches and button toggle indicators where a circle directly mirrors the physical artifact (e.g., horn buttons, mother-of-pearl closures, thread spools).

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Atelier Button**: Deep Midnight Navy background (`#0B1623`), Warm Ivory text (`#F6F1E8`), zero radius, 52px height. Inner padding: `0 36px`. Typography: `label-uppercase`. Hover state shifts surface to Antique Gold (`#B39156`) with text transitioning to Midnight Navy over a 250ms ease.
- **Secondary Basted Button**: Transparent background, 1px solid border in Deep Midnight Navy (`#0B1623`), Midnight Navy text. Hover fills background with Deep Midnight Navy and changes text to Warm Ivory.
- **Tertiary Link**: `label-uppercase` text in Midnight Navy or Antique Gold, paired with an animated 1px underline that expands from left to right on hover.

### Textile & Swatch Selectors
- **Fabric Swatches**: Square 64x64px or circular 48px tactile containers showcasing high-resolution weave textures (Jamdani floral motifs, Prince of Wales check, herringbone wool). Default state framed by a 1px border in Muted Stone. Active state surrounded by a 1px Antique Gold outer bounding box with a 3px Warm Ivory separation gap.
- **Hover Micro-Dossier**: Hovering a swatch triggers a compact popover detailing fabric weight (e.g., *Super 150s Merino Wool - 280g/m - Biella, Italy*).

### Form Controls & Measurement Inputs
- **Input Fields**: Zero-radius text inputs with Warm Ivory or Crisp White fill. Bounded on all four sides by a 1px Muted Stone border, or configured as an editorial single bottom baseline rule (`border-bottom: 1px solid #A59E92`). Label sits floating above in `label-uppercase`. Focused state shifts border to Antique Gold (`#B39156`).
- **Radio & Check Controls**: Custom square selectors (16x16px) with a 1px Midnight Navy border. Selected state displays an inset square of Antique Gold. Never use default browser controls.

### Atelier Garment Dossier Cards
- Composed with strict vertical hierarchy: 4:5 portrait imagery of the cut, separated from meta-information by a 1px Muted Stone divider.
- Base background: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`). Padding: `space-lg`.
- Top metadata row: Origin stamp and cut type (e.g., *DHAKA ATELIER / TWO-PIECE BESPOKE*) set in `label-uppercase` in Muted Stone.
- Card title set in `headline-md` Playfair Display, with pricing and yarn super-count set in `body-sm`.

### Bespoke Configurator Controls
- **Split-Screen Studio View**: Left 7 columns pinned for full-height high-fidelity interactive mannequin render; right 5 columns scrollable for step-by-step lapel, button stance, pocket flap, and lining selections.
- **Step Navigation**: Minimalist horizontal stepper with hairline gold rules connecting numeric markers (`01`, `02`, `03`) set in `label-numeric`.
