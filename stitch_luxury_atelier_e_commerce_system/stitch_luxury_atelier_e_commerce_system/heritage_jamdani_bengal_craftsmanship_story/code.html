The Shitalakshya River Microclimate

True 300-count Jamdani cannot be woven in dry air. The unique morning dew point, relative humidity (78%–84%), and alluvial river mist of Narayanganj keep gossamer Phuti Karpas cotton fibers flexible enough to prevent snap fractures under heavy loom tensions.

Unlike industrial jacquard or print, Jamdani motifs are woven directly into the fabric matrix using fine ox-bone or bamboo needles (kandur). Each motif—from the imperial Panna Hajar to cascading Jaldar—floats weightlessly on pure mulberry silk.
